How it works

  1. Reads the Coinbase Tokenized Stocks API (358 stocks, no key) and Chainlink total-return NAV feeds on Base for the 10 majors.
  2. Scores each stock: momentum over 3 cycles (clipped ±25%), with a trend filter (never overweight a name below its moving average).
  3. Targets equal weights tilted by score, capped at 20% per position, 25% turnover per cycle, 5% cash reserve.
  4. Fills paper trades at NAV, logs every decision with its reasons, and mirrors each trade to the Sepolia TradeJournal.

Paper trading only. This agent does not trade real stocks, hold real funds, or give investment advice. Not available to US users.
